# 🎨 ResumeCraft — 8 Professional Templates

Your resume builder now includes **8 distinct templates**, each optimized for different industries and preferences.

---

## Template Showcase

### 1. **Classic** ✅
- **Style**: Traditional with colored header
- **Font Size**: Medium (1.6rem name)
- **Layout**: Full-width single column
- **Best For**: Corporate, finance, traditional industries
- **Features**: Bold gradient header, centered contact info, clean sections

### 2. **Modern** ✅
- **Style**: Two-column sidebar layout
- **Font Size**: Medium (1.25rem name)
- **Layout**: Colored left sidebar + white main content
- **Best For**: Tech, design, creative fields
- **Features**: Skills in sidebar, vibrant accent color, professional spacing

### 3. **Minimal** ✅
- **Style**: Clean, centered, minimalist
- **Font Size**: Medium (1.5rem name)
- **Layout**: Centered header with bordered entries
- **Best For**: Academic, research, minimalist preference
- **Features**: Left-border accents, maximum whitespace, elegant typography

### 4. **Bold** 🆕
- **Style**: Large fonts, spacious layout
- **Font Size**: Extra Large (2.2rem name)
- **Layout**: Gradient header, generous spacing throughout
- **Best For**: Creative professionals, visual impact seekers
- **Features**: 2.2rem name, big fonts for readability, prominent section titles, spacious margins

### 5. **Executive** 🆕
- **Style**: Premium dark header, corporate elegance
- **Font Size**: Large (1.85rem name)
- **Layout**: Dark header bar, clean white body
- **Best For**: C-suite, management, senior roles
- **Features**: Dark premium header, section borders, professional serif feel, refined styling

### 6. **Creative** 🆕
- **Style**: Two-tone split with vibrant accent
- **Font Size**: Medium-Large (1.4rem name)
- **Layout**: Colored left sidebar vs white right column
- **Best For**: Designers, creatives, artists, marketers
- **Features**: Split-screen design, diagonal visual interest, skills in sidebar, modern vibes

### 7. **Compact** 🆕
- **Style**: ATS-friendly, dense information
- **Font Size**: Small (1.1rem name)
- **Layout**: Minimalist spacing, packed efficiently
- **Best For**: Online job boards, ATS scanning, space-conscious resumes
- **Features**: Reduced font sizes, minimal whitespace, machine-friendly format, dense layout

### 8. **Timeline** 🆕
- **Style**: Vertical timeline with dots and connecting line
- **Font Size**: Medium (1.5rem name)
- **Layout**: Timeline-style experience/education with visual dots
- **Best For**: Visual storytellers, chronological emphasis
- **Features**: Vertical timeline line, dot markers for entries, modern visual storytelling, clean connections

---

## How to Switch Templates

1. Fill your resume details in the form
2. Go to **Step 6: Extras**
3. Under "Resume Template", click any button:
   - **Classic** | **Modern** | **Minimal** | **Bold** | **Executive** | **Creative** | **Compact** | **Timeline**
4. Watch your resume update instantly in the preview!

## Which Template Should I Use?

- **Big Fonts? Pick Bold** — Name in 2.2rem, spacious design
- **ATS Scanning? Pick Compact** — Tight layout, machine-friendly
- **Visual Impact? Pick Creative or Timeline** — Unique designs
- **Traditional Job? Pick Classic or Executive** — Professional, corporate
- **Tech/Design? Pick Modern** — Sidebar layout, visual balance
- **Minimalist? Pick Minimal** — Clean, elegant, simple
